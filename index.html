School of Computer Science and Technology

Hefei, China

I work as an Associate Professor at the BIMK Research Group in School of Computer Science and Technology, Anhui University in China.

I graduated from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Anhui University with a bachelor’s degree and a doctoral degree. I was also a Visiting Ph.D. student at Bielefeld University, Germany in 2022.

My research interests include automated machine learning, neural architecture search, and evolutionary computation for smart education applications. I have authored/coauthored 50+ peer-reviewed papers, with 30+ papers published in prestigious IEEE Trans. (TNNLS, TEVC, TETCI, etc.) and top-tier conferences (NeurIPS, AAAI, IJCAI, KDD, SIGIR, etc.). I have received the National Scholarship for Postgraduate (2019), multiple best paper/poster awards at top conferences, and the Postdoctoral Fellowship Program (Grade B) of the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation.
